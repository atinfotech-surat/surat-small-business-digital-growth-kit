# Contributing

Thank you for helping make the kit more useful to small businesses.

## Good contributions

- Plain-language improvements and examples that work for small teams.
- Gujarati translations reviewed by a fluent speaker; include the reviewer or review process where they agree.
- Corrections to outdated platform guidance with a link to the current official source.
- Industry-specific examples clearly labeled as examples—not promises or universal rules.
- Accessibility improvements and easier-to-print formatting.

## Please don't submit

- Customer records, personal phone numbers, private addresses, account credentials, or confidential client material.
- Client logos, screenshots, photos, testimonials, or results without written permission.
- Fake reviews, keyword-stuffed business names, guaranteed ranking claims, or incentivized-review scripts.
- Medical, financial, legal, or safety claims presented as professional advice.

## How to propose a change

1. Open an issue describing the resource and the problem or improvement.
2. Link sources for platform-specific or factual claims; prefer official primary sources.
3. Keep advice practical for a small team and explain when a step is optional.
4. For language changes, have a fluent reviewer check meaning, tone, and local usage before merging.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution can be shared under the project's CC BY 4.0 license unless a file says otherwise.
