# Sources and further reading

Links checked on 13 October 2026. Platform interfaces and policies can change; check the linked official guidance before acting.

## Google Business Profile and Maps

- [Tips to improve your local ranking on Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en) — Google's explanation of local results, including relevance, distance, and prominence. It states that businesses cannot request or pay Google for a better local organic ranking.
- [Guidelines for representing your business on Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en) — business identity, address/service area, categories, and profile representation.
- [Prohibited and restricted content: fake and misleading content and reviews](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114?hl=en) — genuine experience, incentives, and rating-manipulation rules. Read the current policy before asking for reviews.

## Website content and search

- [Google Search: SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide) — basic guidance on useful, discoverable website content and Search Console.
- [Google Search Console: Get started](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/search-console-start) — ownership verification, indexing checks, and performance reports.

## Accessibility

- [W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) — the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines.
- [W3C WCAG 2.2 Quick Reference](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/) — criteria and techniques to use when planning or reviewing web content.

## How to use these references

This kit summarizes practical starting points; it is not an official Google or W3C publication. Platform guidance is not a guarantee of rankings, eligibility, traffic, leads, or sales. For legal/privacy questions or formal accessibility conformance, seek qualified advice appropriate to the business and jurisdiction.
