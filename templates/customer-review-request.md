# Requesting customer feedback—without pressure

Use a review request only after a genuine transaction or service experience. Keep it neutral, optional, and the same for eligible customers. Don't offer money, discounts, gifts, or other benefits for reviews; don't ask only customers you expect to be happy; don't request a specific rating or wording. Check the current platform policy in [`../SOURCES.md`](../SOURCES.md).

## WhatsApp / SMS

> Hi [Name], thank you for choosing [Business]. If you’d like to share honest feedback about your experience, you can leave it here: [review link]. It’s completely optional. Your feedback helps us understand what’s working and what we can improve. Thank you.

## Email

**Subject:** How was your experience with [Business]?

> Hello [Name],
>
> Thank you for choosing [Business] for [service/order]. If you have a moment, we welcome an honest review of your experience: [review link]. There’s no pressure to leave one, and any feedback—positive or critical—helps us improve.
>
> Thank you,  
> [Team / Business]

## Staff reminder

- Send the same neutral request to customers according to a consistent process.
- Do not filter people based on whether they seem satisfied.
- Do not ask customers to leave a review while they are under pressure at the counter.
- Do not ask them to include a particular phrase, rating, photo, or employee name.
- Do not share an order number, phone number, health detail, or other personal information in a public response.

## Replying to feedback

**Positive:** Thank the person and mention one appropriate detail without exposing private information.

**Critical:** Thank them for raising the concern; acknowledge the experience without arguing; offer a suitable private route to investigate. Do not ask them to remove or change a review in exchange for a benefit.

**Example response:**

> Thank you for sharing this, [Name]. We’re sorry the experience didn’t meet expectations. We’d like to understand what happened and see what we can do. Please contact us at [public contact route] and mention [non-sensitive reference, if appropriate].

Adapt the wording to your actual process. Never promise a refund, replacement, or outcome that your team cannot deliver.
