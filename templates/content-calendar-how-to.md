# How to use the 30-day content calendar

The companion CSV is a bank of ideas—not a rule to publish every day. Choose a pace your team can sustain, such as two or three useful updates a week, and adapt each idea to your own business.

## A simple workflow

1. Pick one business goal for the month (for example, explain a service, announce seasonal hours, or help customers choose a product).
2. Choose 8–12 ideas from the calendar; skip ideas that don't fit.
3. Pick the channel your customers actually use. One good post adapted for a second channel is better than copying everywhere without context.
4. Add a planned date, owner, and asset. Check prices, dates, links, and claims before publishing.
5. Use one clear call to action. Make it easy to contact, visit, book, or learn more.
6. At month-end, record which posts prompted useful questions or enquiries; plan from that learning.

## Suggested content mix

- **Helpful:** answer a real question, explain a choice, or share a care/use tip.
- **Proof:** show a real process, team, product, or approved customer story.
- **Practical:** hours, service area, ordering, booking, delivery, or availability.
- **Community:** an event or local collaboration, only with permission and accurate details.
- **Offer:** share a genuine offer with clear terms and dates; don't create fake scarcity.

## Before posting

- [ ] Is it accurate and still current?
- [ ] Do we own the photo/video or have permission to use it?
- [ ] Do people shown know where and how the image will be used?
- [ ] Does the post avoid exposing private customer details?
- [ ] Is the CTA a real action we can support?
- [ ] If mentioning a result or testimonial, can we verify it and use it with approval?

You don't have to use every prompt. Consistency and usefulness matter more than posting volume.
