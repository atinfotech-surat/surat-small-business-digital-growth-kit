# Surat Small-Business Digital Growth Kit

**Practical, no-code guides and reusable templates for small businesses in Surat and Gujarat.**

Edition 1.0 · 13 October 2026 · Prepared by ATinfotech

This kit helps an owner or small team plan a clearer website, keep business information accurate online, create useful content, and review enquiries without expensive tools. It is written for local retailers, food businesses, professional services, studios, and manufacturers. You do not need to write code to use it.

## Start here

1. **Choose one goal** for the next month: more qualified enquiries, bookings, store visits, or repeat orders.
2. Fill in [`templates/website-content-brief.md`](templates/website-content-brief.md). Use it even if you are not building a new website yet.
3. Work through the [website launch checklist](guides/01-website-launch-checklist.md) and the [local search profile guide](guides/02-local-search-profile-guide.md).
4. Adapt the [30-day content calendar](templates/30-day-content-calendar.csv); you do not have to post every day.
5. At month-end, use the [digital check-up](guides/03-monthly-digital-checkup.md) to choose one improvement for the next month.

## A few principles

- Be accurate about your business name, location, hours, prices, services, and delivery promises.
- Use real images and real customer feedback, with permission. Never invent testimonials, results, or reviews.
- Do not offer a discount, gift, or other incentive in exchange for a Google review. Ask neutrally for an honest review; don't ask only happy customers.
- Collect only customer information you need, store it safely, and don't put personal information in a public repository.
- Track your own baseline. This kit does not guarantee search rankings, leads, sales, or a particular growth result.
- Platform rules change. Check the official references in [`SOURCES.md`](SOURCES.md) before relying on platform-specific guidance.
